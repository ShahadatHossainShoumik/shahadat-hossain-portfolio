Summary

I am Shahadat Hossain, a Software Engineer, Data Analyst and AI Researcher completing my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at AIUB with the highest academic distinction (CGPA 3.97/4.00). Supported by a 70% merit scholarship, five consecutive Dean’s List awards and a fully funded Student Mobility fellowship in Malaysia, I specialise in scalable full-stack applications, predictive machine-learning models and data-driven software systems.

Across 15+ academic and technical projects I have worked across the full stack of software and data engineering — cleaning 350,000+ meteorological records in Python, architecting MVC web platforms in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, building role-based desktop applications in C# (.NET 8), and designing user-tested interfaces in Figma and Power BI. My passion for AI, Machine Learning and Explainable AI (XAI) led me to publish a Scopus-indexed IEEE conference paper (AeroLung AI) and to pursue research targeting Q1-indexed journals.

In June 2026 I was one of only five students from Bangladesh selected for a fully funded place on the INTI Student Mobility Programme in Malaysia, collaborating with 216 students from 38 universities across 10 countries. It taught me to adapt fast, bridge diverse perspectives and turn complex challenges into clean, impactful technology — and I want to keep doing exactly that with teams that value innovation, integrity and continuous growth.

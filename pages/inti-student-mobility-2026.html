International Student Mobility Exchange Programme Experience

In June 2026, in recognition of my academic standing and extracurricular involvement, I was nominated by American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB) for a fully funded scholarship to attend the Student Mobility Programme at INTI International University, Malaysia which is one of the region’s premier institutions, holding a QS World University Ranking of #406. Out of the entire country, only five students from Bangladesh were selected for the June 2026 cohort, and I had the immense honour of being one of them.

Stepping onto a global academic platform alongside 216 students from 38 universities across 10 countries—including Indonesia, India, Uzbekistan, Pakistan, Vietnam, Nigeria, the Philippines, Kazakhstan, Cambodia, and Bangladesh—was both humbling and transformative. Moving to a new country and adjusting to an unfamiliar academic system surrounded by people I had never met was initially challenging. However, pushing through that discomfort taught me rapid adaptability, empathy, and how to collaborate across diverse cultures and perspectives.

During the semester, I completed Introduction to Data Analytics, Human-Computer Interface (HCI), and Software Quality under distinguished professors. Despite the transition of living and studying abroad for the first time, I stayed focused, worked relentlessly, and earned A grades across my modules, finishing the semester with an SGPA of 3.88 out of 4.00.

Beyond the classroom, I actively took part in campus leadership and cultural exchange initiatives, and spent my weekends and semester breaks exploring the vibrant landscapes and heritage of Malaysia. Looking back, my time at INTI proved to me that no environment is too unfamiliar and nothing is impossible when backed by resilience, willpower, and hard work.

AIUB Students Participate in Student Mobility Program at INTI International University, Malaysia

Published Monday, June 1, 2026 · American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)

“Five AIUB students have joined the Student Mobility Program at INTI International University, Malaysia, for the June 2026 intake. The one-semester exchange (June–August 2026) will allow students to complete coursework and earn 7–10 credit hours.”

“Representing diverse faculties, the participants are: Md Shaheduzzaman (Faculty of Business Administration), enrolled in INTI’s Bachelor of Accounting and Finance (Hons); Md Arif Mahmud Oyion, Shahadat Hossain, and Mubassir Islam Jimel (Faculty of Science and Technology), enrolled in the Bachelor of Computer Science (Hons); and Anika Tahsin Alif (Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences), enrolled in the Bachelor of Mass Communication (Hons).”

“During the exchange, students will study contemporary modules including Artificial Intelligence, IT Project Management, Introduction to Data Analytics, Software Quality Assurance, Advanced Corporate Finance, Derivative Markets, Media Ethics, and Media and Society.”

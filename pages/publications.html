AeroLung AI: An Explainable Machine Learning Framework for Lung Cancer Prediction

Authors: Md. Shamsul Alam, Shahadat Hossain, Hamidul Islam, Sharfuddin Mahmood, Md. Osman Goni

Presented at the 2026 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence and Networking (QPAIN 2026), held on 16–18 April 2026 at the IT Business Incubator, Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Chattogram, Bangladesh, and organized by the IEEE Photonics Society Bangladesh Chapter.

AeroLung AI introduces an explainable machine-learning pipeline for early lung cancer risk prediction, combining ensemble classification with post-hoc interpretability so clinicians and patients can inspect the key symptom and demographic drivers behind every prediction.

Publication 02 · ICETSD 2025 — JUST, Bangladesh
Conference Paper Computational Intelligence & Applications 22–23 Feb 2025

Cybercrime Victimization and Enforcement Challenges in Bangladesh: A Socio-Cybersecurity Perspective (2018–2023)

Presented by: Shahadat Hossain · American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)

Presented at the International Conference on Emerging Technologies for Sustainable Development (ICETSD 2025), held on 22–23 February 2025 in Jashore, Bangladesh, and organized by the Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST).

This study investigates longitudinal patterns of cybercrime victimization and institutional enforcement bottlenecks in Bangladesh between 2018 and 2023, offering a socio-cybersecurity framework for strengthening digital literacy, victim reporting pathways, and cyber law enforcement capacity.

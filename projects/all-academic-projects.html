Hotel Booking Analytics and Customer Retention Study

Facebook Website UI Redesign Prototype

Project Module: User-centred redesign of four Facebook website pages: Home Feed, Registration, Friend Request, and Notifications

Technologies and Methods: Figma prototype; Google Forms close-ended questionnaires; user personas; hypothesis formulation; before-and-after usability testing with 1-5 ratings

Course and University: HCI3202 Human Computer Interface, INTI International University and Colleges

Final Outcome: Surveyed 30 respondents on the existing interface and redesigned four pages in Figma. In the follow-up survey, the redesigned pages were rated between 4.03 and 4.68 out of 5.

Digital Touchscreen UI Redesign for the INTI Vending Machine

Key Academic Project

Project Module: Eight-screen digital touchscreen interface for a self-service vending machine

Technologies and Methods: Figma prototype; Google Forms survey with closed-ended, Likert-scale, and one open-ended question; Nielsen’s usability heuristics

Software Quality Assurance of Meta’s Facebook Platform

Project Module: Change, version, system building, and release management analysis of Facebook quality failures

Technologies and Methods: Figma prototype; ISO 9126; McCall’s quality factors; IEEE defect classification; change request form; Git, GitHub, and GitLab (recommended)

Course and University: SDT4202 Software Quality, INTI International University and Colleges

Project Supervisor: Dr. Noor Zuhaili Md Yasin, Professor, INTI International University.

Final Outcome: Analysed four Facebook failures from 2018 to 2024, proposed a seven-stage change management model, designed a Figma prototype of a Facebook clone, and recommended Agile development.

Meta Facebook SQA
